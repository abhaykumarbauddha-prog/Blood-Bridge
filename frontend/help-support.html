📚 Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to donate blood? −
Generally, anyone in good health, aged 18-65 years, and weighing at least 50 kg can donate blood. You must not have donated in the last 3 months (90 days). Specific medical conditions or recent travel may affect eligibility.
How do I create a blood request? +
Navigate to the 'Blood Requests' tab in your sidebar, click 'Create Request', and fill in the patient details, required blood type, hospital location, and urgency level.
What is the Cooling Period? +
The cooling period is the mandatory waiting time between donations to allow your body to replenish its red blood cells. It is typically 90 days for men and 120 days for women.
How does the matching system work? +
BloodMatch uses your location and registered blood type to instantly notify you when a compatible patient within a 100km radius requires a blood transfusion.
Is my personal information secure? +
Yes, your data is protected using enterprise-grade security protocols powered by Supabase Row Level Security. Only necessary details (like blood group and distance) are shown to requesters.
